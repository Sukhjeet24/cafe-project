Page 05 — Poem Corner
This Week's Poem: "Steam"
The kettle exhales before I do,
a small white ghost above the cup,
and somewhere in that rising breath
a sentence I haven't written yet.

I read slower than I pour.
The page waits. The cup cools.
Both of us, in our own way,
learning to be patient with warmth.
— Posted by the morning shift, every Sunday
